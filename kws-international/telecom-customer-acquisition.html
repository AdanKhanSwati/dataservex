Customer acquisition

Telecom customer acquisition with process behind it.

KWS International approaches customer acquisition as an operating system: source the prospect, qualify the need, position the offer, capture the order accurately and protect the customer experience.

Multi-channel customer acquisition

Different telecom offers require different sales motions. KWS International is built to work across telesales, digital lead response, field acquisition and in-person engagement while maintaining a consistent standard for qualification and communication.

What the process covers

  • Lead and prospect intake
  • Needs discovery and serviceability checks
  • Offer and package positioning
  • Accurate customer and order information
  • Expectation-setting before handoff
  • Quality review and coaching

Residential and small-business conversations

Our team experience includes consumer and SMB telecom conversations involving wireless, internet, broadband, video and connectivity products. Specific carrier authorization is never implied unless established separately by formal agreement.
